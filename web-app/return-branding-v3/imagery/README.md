# Imagery

Painted environments for heroes, section backgrounds, the VR hub and empty states. All are 2048×1152 WebP, one consistent hand-painted matte-painting style. The original PNGs are in the branding kit.

Day theme:
- `return-sky-day-meadow-lake.webp`: blossom meadow, mirror lake, big cumulus. The landing hero.
- `return-sky-day-golden-plain.webp`: golden plain under a towering cloud, a tiny figure walking away. "How it works", onboarding.
- `return-sky-day-hilltop-home.webp`: a white house on a green hill. Families, "coming home" moments, sample room.
- `return-sky-day-morning-beach.webp`: pastel morning beach with a lifeguard tower. Sample room, summer.
- `return-sky-day-cloud-field.webp`: pure pastel clouds, no horizon. Quiet backgrounds behind PhotoDrop, sheets and sign-in; the safest place for text.
- `return-sky-golden-painted-clouds.webp`: classical oil-painted sky, golden light. Editorial sections, the manifesto, share previews.

Dusk theme:
- `return-sky-dusk-cloud-sea.webp`: a figure on a lavender hill above a sea of clouds with a glowing river. Dusk hero, "together" moments.
- `return-sky-dusk-night-lake.webp`: starry night over a still lake. The VR hub skybox mood, night viewing, the dusk empty state.

Headline text goes over open sky or haze, never over busy detail, always with the built-in scrims. Crop with `object-fit: cover` and keep the horizon in the lower third.
