# ScreenRoomUpload

Route `/rooms/:id/add`. Step 2 of 3. Each person adds their photos of the place and one note. Invited people land here after joining.

- Left: breadcrumb, Stepper (step 2), "Add your *view* of it", PhotoDrop (up to 12, first is the cover), Field (multiline) "Leave a note for this room".
- Right: "In this *room*" panel with MemberList (live) and Invite someone else.
- Primary: "Add to the room" → uploads, then screen 6. "Save for later" keeps a draft.
