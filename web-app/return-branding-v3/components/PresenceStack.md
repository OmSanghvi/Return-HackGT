# PresenceStack

Overlapping initials for the people connected to a room; people inside it right now glow `aurora`.

Props: `people` (`[{name, here}]`), `max` (default 4, the rest collapse to +N), `size` (`sm` | `md` | `lg`), `showLabel` ("N here now"), `onImage` (glass avatars for use on cards and heroes).

- `aurora` means another person, everywhere in return. Don't use it for anything else.
